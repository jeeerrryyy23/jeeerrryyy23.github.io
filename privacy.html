Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Hermes Morning Brief is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): recent messages in the owner's own inboxes, to find event-related emails.
  • Google Calendar (events): the owner's own calendar, to check for duplicates and to add events the owner explicitly approves.

How data is used and stored

  • Email content is processed only to compose the owner's daily summary. Full emails are not stored.
  • Access tokens are kept only on the owner's computer.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
